Easescan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mastertech CA, Inc. ("we", "our", or "the Company") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use the Easescan mobile application and its associated OBD2 Bluetooth hardware scanner.

1. Required App Permissions

For Easescan to function correctly across its different stages, we request access to the following device features:

  • Camera: Required exclusively in Stage 1 to scan your license activation QR code. Images are not saved or transmitted; QR code processing is done locally on your device.
  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices: Required in Stage 2 to discover and establish local communication with the Easescan hardware scanner plugged into your vehicle's ECU.
  • Location: On Android versions below 12, the operating system requires location permissions to scan for Bluetooth devices. Easescan does not track, collect, or store your GPS location on our servers under any circumstances.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

The application's operation is divided into local services and cloud services, handling the following data:

A. License Activation (Firebase)

When you scan your QR code and connect to the Bluetooth scanner, the app links your hardware to your device to activate your permanent license. During this process, we securely transmit the following to our cloud servers (Google Firebase/Firestore):

  • The activation code (QR).
  • The internal serial number of the Easescan hardware.
  • Unique device identifiers (such as SSAID / GUUID).
  • Warranty and license status dates.

Purpose: This data is used strictly for anti-piracy measures, to validate your license, and to grant access to premium features.

B. Automotive Diagnostics & AI Assistance (Cloudflare)

When the app reads Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) from your vehicle, they are processed locally. However, if you choose to use the "AI Diagnostic" button (Stage 3), the app will send the following to our cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare):

  • The specific fault code (e.g., P0165).
  • Your temporary License Token to validate an active subscription.

Purpose: This information is used exclusively to generate a structured diagnostic report (description, causes, symptoms, and solutions). We do not associate your vehicle's fault codes with your personal identity, nor do we sell diagnostic histories to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We share strictly necessary technical information with the following infrastructure providers, all of which comply with high security standards:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC): For license authentication and real-time database management.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: For processing our Artificial Intelligence API (Workers / D1 Database) and delivering diagnostic reports.

All data transmission to these servers is conducted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your active license record in our databases for the lifetime of the product to ensure your hardware and app work seamlessly together. If you wish to revoke your license, unlink your mobile device, or request the complete deletion of your identifiers from our system, you may exercise your right by contacting our support team.

5. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy occasionally to reflect app improvements or legal changes. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app update or by changing the date at the top of this page.
