Account & Data Deletion Policy

App Name: Easescan

Developer: Mastertech CA, Inc.

At Mastertech, we value your privacy. If you no longer wish to use the EaseScan application and want to permanently delete your account and associated data, you can do so at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

You have two easy ways to delete your account:

Method 1: Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Easescan application on your Android device.
  2. Ensure you are signed in with the Google Account you wish to delete.
  3. Tap on the Menu at the bottom of the screen, and navigate to the About section.
  4. Locate the Account Management card.
  5. Tap the red Delete Account button.
  6. Confirm your choice in the pop-up dialog. Your account will be instantly and permanently deleted, and you will be signed out.

Method 2: Via Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer to request deletion manually, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to info@easescan.com from the email address associated with your EaseScan account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. In the body of the email, state that you wish to permanently delete your EaseScan account.
  4. Our support team will process your request and delete your account within 14 business days.

What Data is Deleted?

When you initiate an account deletion, the following data is permanently erased from our active Firebase Authentication and Firestore servers:

  • ✗ Your associated Google Account Email Address.
  • ✗ Your Firebase User ID (UID).
  • ✗ The link associating your email address with your specific EaseScan Bluetooth Hardware device.

What Data is Retained?

To prevent hardware cloning fraud and to honor warranty periods, we retain the following anonymized device metadata indefinitely on our servers:

  • ✓ The physical Hardware Serial Number (easescanserial).
  • ✓ The hardware Manufacture Date and Warranty Expiration Date.
  • ✓ The activation status of the physical hardware (e.g., whether the device is marked as "Active," "Lost," or "Revoked").

Note: Once your account is deleted, this retained hardware data is completely anonymized and can no longer be linked back to you, your email address, or your identity.
