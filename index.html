Glossary of Terms

BAROMETRIC PRESSURE

Atmospheric pressure reading used by the engine computer for fuel calculations.

DLC

Data Link Connector - The OBD2 port where you connect the scanner.

DTC

Diagnostic Trouble Code - The fault code stored in your vehicle's computer.

ECM

Engine Control Module - The main computer that controls the engine.

FUEL PRESSURE

The pressure of fuel in the fuel system, critical for proper engine operation.

IAT

Intake Air Temperature - Sensor measuring air temperature进入 the engine.

LONG TERM BANK 1

Long-term fuel trim adjustments made by the computer over time.

MAF

Mass Air Flow - Sensor measuring the amount of air entering the engine.

MAP

Manifold Absolute Pressure - Sensor measuring pressure in the intake manifold.

OBD2

On-Board Diagnostics II - Standard diagnostic system for vehicles.

RPM

Revolutions Per Minute - Engine speed measurement.

SAE

Society of Automotive Engineers - Organization setting automotive standards.

SHORT TERM BANK 1

Short-term fuel trim adjustments made by the computer in real-time.

TPS

Throttle Position Sensor - Sensor measuring throttle plate position.
